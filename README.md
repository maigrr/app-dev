# Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban
**Author:** J.K. Rowling  
**Published:** 1999  
**Genre:** Fantasy, Adventure  

---

## Summary

*Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban* is the third book in the **Harry Potter** series.  
It follows Harry as he returns to Hogwarts for his third year, only to discover that a dangerous prisoner named **Sirius Black** has escaped from Azkaban and is supposedly after him.

---
